About Me

From Curiosity to Career Direction

Hi, I am Tran Quoc Van, a third-year Digital Economy student at RMIT University, born and raised in Ben Tre — Vietnam’s “Land of Coconuts.”

My journey has not been completely linear. I initially studied Biology, driven by curiosity about nature and ecosystems, before realising that I was more energised by business challenges, technology, people, and dynamic environments. Moving into the business field has become the starting point for my discovery journey where my strengths could create the greatest value.

Throughout RMIT, I have intentionally explored different environments through academic projects, business competitions, student leadership, and industry engagement. These experiences have strengthened my capabilities in strategic thinking, stakeholder management, communication, and project leadership, while gradually shaping my interest in Business Development and Commercial Strategy.

More importantly, my perspective has evolved from seeking opportunities for myself to creating value and opportunities for others. Today, I approach growth with three principles:

  • Curiosity - continuously learn from unfamiliar challenges.
  • Community Value-driven — focus on meaningful outcomes rather than activities alone.
  • Grow Through People — build trust, collaborate, and enable others to contribute.

Leadership in Practice

One experience shaped my leadership philosophy more than any event metric.

When Ánh first joined the department, she was extremely quiet and found it difficult to express herself confidently. Rather than viewing this as a limitation, I believed that the right environment and level of responsibility could help her grow.

I encouraged her to become Project Leader of the Finance 101 Workshop and initially supported her in project management, communication, and stakeholder coordination.

As her confidence developed, I gradually stepped back.

Within three months, I saw her become a more confident, proactive, and independent contributor.

What It Changed in Me

"Leadership is not about changing people. It is about creating an environment where people can discover and develop their own potential."

This shifted my leadership approach from directing people toward enabling ownership and growth.
